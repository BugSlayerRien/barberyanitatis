Appointment time and scheduling validations (mobile flow)

1) Entry point and source of truth
- Mobile sends booking requests to booking.php (action=create).
- Final validation is enforced on backend, so all roles and clients follow the same rules.

2) Date and time format validation
- appointmentDate must be valid YYYY-MM-DD.
- startTime and endTime are normalized to HH:MM:SS.
- If date/time format is invalid, request is rejected with 422.

3) Time logic and duration checks
- endTime must be strictly later than startTime.
- Duration is computed from start and end.
- Allowed range is 10 to 240 minutes.
- Past appointments are blocked (start datetime must be in the future).

4) Account and role integrity checks
- userID must point to an active customer account.
- barberID must point to an active barber account.

5) Scheduling conflict checks
- Customer cannot have another active appointment (Scheduled, CheckedIn, InProgress).
- Barber cannot have overlapping active appointments for the same date/time range.

6) Slot availability checks and synchronization
- The selected start slot must exist in time_slots and be Available.
- On successful booking, that slot is updated to Booked.
- On cancellation, the slot is released back to Available.
- On receptionist status update:
	- Cancelled/NoShow => slot becomes Available
	- Other active states => slot stays Booked

7) Why this works in mobile too
- Mobile UI may guide the user, but backend decides final validity.
- This prevents race conditions and inconsistent schedules across customer, receptionist, and barber views.
